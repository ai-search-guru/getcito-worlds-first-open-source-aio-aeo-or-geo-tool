# Rival brief

A one-page brief on one competitor in AI answers: where they lead, the prompts they win, the pages AI cites for them, how AI describes them, and what the brand can do.

## When to use

- "Tell me about Northwind in AI search", "Why does AI recommend them over us?", "Competitor moves."
- To work on one prompt they win, the Beat a competitor skill fits better.

## What to read

1. `list_competitors`: the tracked competitors. When the person names none, ask, with these as the options.
2. `get_competitor_comparison` for the period: where they lead, per platform.
3. `get_visibility_trend`: how their visibility and the brand's moved.
4. `get_prompt_performance` and `list_ai_answers`: the prompts they win, and the answers that recommend them.
5. `list_citations` with the competitor filter: the pages AI cites for them.
6. `get_mention_competitors` and `list_mentions`: how AI describes them beside the brand.

## Steps

1. Summarise where they stand against the brand in three lines.
2. By platform: who leads, and by how much.
3. The prompts they win that the brand doesn't, with the pages AI cites for them there.
4. How AI describes them, quoted, and what it credits them with.
5. Three moves for the brand, each tied to a prompt or a page.

## Output

1. One page: Summary, By platform, Prompts they win, Pages AI cites for them, How AI describes them, What to do next.
2. Every number with the period it covers.

## Don't

- Don't disparage the competitor, and claim nothing about them a read page or answer doesn't say.
- Don't copy their text: say what their pages answer.
- Never promise the moves will win.
