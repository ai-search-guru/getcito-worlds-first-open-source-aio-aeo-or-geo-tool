## What is GetCito?

[GetCito](https://getcito.com) is an open-source, cloud-ready **AI Search Visibility, GEO, & AEO** platform that helps brands measure and improve their visibility across AI-powered search and answer engines.

Track prompt visibility, citations, rankings, competitor benchmarks, and brand performance across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews.

GetCito enables teams to turn AI search into a measurable growth channel with optimization insights, visibility reporting, competitive intelligence, and data-driven content workflows powered by MCP Server.

### What can you do with GetCito?

- Learn growth opportunities in one click.
- Discover Prompt Volumes and get new Prompt and Topic suggestions.
- Monitor visibility and citations across major AI engines.
- Benchmark competitors and track AI search performance.
- Generate GEO & AEO optimization insights and briefs.
- Build long-term authority across AI search ecosystems.

## Open-source and self-hostable

GetCito is MIT-licensed and can run on your own infrastructure. The source and license notices are in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ai-search-guru/getcito-worlds-first-open-source-aio-aeo-or-geo-tool).

These docs use a local deployment for setup examples. References to **cloud mode** describe optional billing features configured by a deployment's operator; they do not announce a hosted service or subscription offer. Self-hosted mode does not require a subscription.
