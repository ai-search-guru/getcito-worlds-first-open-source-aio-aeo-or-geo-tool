## What is the Topics view?

The **Topics** view (**Prompts → Topics**, `/dashboard/prompts/topics`; the old `/dashboard/topics` address redirects there) groups your tracked prompts by theme so you can see AEO performance at a higher level than individual prompts. The date range offers 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d or custom, and opens on 24 hours.

At the top, four cards sum up your topics:

- **Tracked topics** — how many topics you have, and how many prompts have no topic yet
- **Best performer** — the topic with the highest Visibility score
- **Biggest gap** — the topic with the lowest score
- **Top mover** — the topic whose score rose most, in points

The **Topic leaderboard** below has a row per topic:

- Topic name and prompt count
- Visibility — the 0–100 score over the topic's answers, the same score as on Brand Visibility
- SoV, Mentions and Citations
- Trend — a small line and the change in points against the window of the same length just before
- Top competitor — the competitor with the largest share of voice in the topic
- Last run
- One-click drill-down into the topic detail page

Click **Prompts**, **Visibility**, **SoV**, **Mentions**, **Citations** or **Last run** to sort by it; the table opens sorted by Visibility.

The buttons beside the tabs are **Add Topic**, **Export CSV** (the leaderboard as a CSV file) and **Manage topics**.

## When to use it

- **Editorial planning** — pick the topic with the lowest score and weakest competitor lead; that's your next content sprint
- **Reporting to stakeholders** — topic-level stories ("we own EV Range, we're losing on Reliability") are easier to communicate than prompt-level metrics
- **Spotting drift** — a topic that drops while others stay flat usually signals a single competitor surge or content rot

## Topic detail page

Clicking into a topic shows all of its results, not only the date range picked on the Topics tab:

- **Visibility**, **Brand mentions**, **Citations** and **Positive sentiment** for the topic
- **Visibility trend** — your daily visibility on the topic against the competitor average
- **Share of voice by platform** — you against competitors on each answer engine
- **Competitor comparison** — the top six brands on this topic, ranked by their score, with yours marked **YOU**
- **Prompt performance** — the topic's **Top performers** and the prompts under **Needs improvement**, from its last 50 runs

**View all prompt results for this topic** opens Brand Visibility filtered to the topic.

## Suggested topics

The **Topic Suggestions** card on the Topics tab starts folded. Open it to see AI-generated topic ideas for your brand; topics you already track and ideas you dismissed don't come back. Admins and managers can **Refresh** for new ideas, add an idea to your topics, or dismiss it. A new topic starts empty: assign prompts to it to start tracking it.

## Editing topics

Only admins and managers can change topics; other roles can view them.

- **Add Topic** creates a new, empty topic.
- To **delete** a topic, open the **…** menu at the end of its row › **Delete topic**. The dialog says how many prompts are in the topic. They aren't deleted: they keep their tracking and results, and move to prompts without a topic. After you confirm, **Undo** puts the topic back for 10 seconds.
- **Manage topics** opens the brand's topic settings, where you can also **rename** a topic (the pencil icon). Its prompts stay attached.

<Tip>
Keep topic names short (2–4 words) and intent-driven. *"EV Range"* is better than *"Electric Vehicle Driving Distance Comparisons"*.
</Tip>
