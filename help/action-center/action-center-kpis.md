The **KPIs** tab tracks the goals you set for the brand. Each KPI shows its **Value**, **Change**, **Trend (7D)**, **Goal**, progress **vs Goal** and a **Status**. An action's Overview lists the KPIs it is linked to.

## KPIs you can track

AI Visibility, Citations, Mentions, Share of Voice, AI Referral Traffic and Positive Sentiment.

A brand that never had KPIs starts with a suggested set: AI Visibility, Citations, Mentions, Share of Voice and Positive Sentiment, plus AI Referral Traffic when Google Analytics is connected. Each aims 20% above its last 30 days, or starts from a default where there's no data yet. It is saved the first time an admin or manager opens the tab.

## KPI status

- **Goal Reached**: 100% of the goal or more.
- **On Track**: 70% or more, and not moving the wrong way.
- **At Risk**: 40 to 69%, or moving the wrong way before the goal is reached.
- **Off Track**: under 40%.

## Change your KPIs

Click **Edit KPIs** (or **Configure KPIs**) to open the framework. Pick a template (**AI Search Visibility**, **Brand Monitoring** or **Custom Framework**; **Content Performance** is marked coming soon), a time period (Weekly, Monthly or Quarterly) and a target for each KPI, then click **Save & Apply**. Only admins and managers can change KPIs.
