The **Reports** page makes point-in-time snapshots of your brand's AI visibility. Unlike the live dashboards, a report never changes: its numbers are captured when it is generated and stay the same as new tracking results arrive. That makes it the right tool for a monthly review, a client deliverable, or a before-and-after comparison around a campaign.

## Generate a report

Click **Generate Report** and pick a template. A template is a default set of sections, and you can switch any section on or off before generating. Then give the report a title if you like, pick the **Brand** and the **Date Range** (last 7, 30 or 90 days, or Custom), and check the **Sections**. Generated reports appear under **Generated Reports** on the same page, newest first. Delete a report with the bin icon there.

## Templates

- **Weekly Visibility Summary**: KPIs, Trend and Share of Voice, over the last 7 days.
- **Monthly Executive Report**: every section, over the last 30 days.
- **Competitor Benchmark**: KPIs, Share of Voice, Competitors and Topics, over the last 30 days.
- **Citation & Sources Report**: Citations and Citation Evidence, over the last 30 days.

## Sections

The full picker offers KPIs, Trend, Share of Voice, Competitors, Topics, Prompts, Mention Evidence, Fan-out, AI Traffic, Product Visibility, Audit Score, Citations and Citation Evidence. **Product Visibility** appears only for brands with Shopping mode on. The AI executive summary is always included: it isn't a section you pick. If it can't be written, the report is saved with its figures and no summary.

## Read a report

A report opens with its **Executive Summary**: a short account of what changed versus the previous period, such as the headline visibility movement, mention and citation growth, and sentiment and competitor shifts. One card per selected section follows, in a fixed order. Each metric is compared with the previous period of equal length, so a 30-day report is compared with the 30 days before it. Percentage changes read +X%, and point changes for visibility and sentiment +X pts. Because the snapshot is frozen, a report's numbers can differ from the live dashboards.

## Download a PDF

**Download PDF** saves the report as a PDF with the same sections as the web view, the executive summary included. Its text stays selectable and sharp at any zoom, and the file is small enough to attach to an email. With white label in use, the report and its PDF carry your agency's logo, color, name and footer instead of GetCito's, with a "Prepared for" cover.
