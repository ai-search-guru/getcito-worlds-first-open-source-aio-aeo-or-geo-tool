Every URL that AI engines cite has its own page in GetCito. Open it from the **URLs** tab of the **Citations** page by clicking a page's title. It opens with the Citations page's date range, platform and region, but not its topic or prompt. You can change the date range and platform on the URL's page. **Citations** at the top goes back.

## The header

The header shows the page's title and address, and its source type. When GetCito can tell from the address and title, it also shows the article type: **Topic Guide**, **How-to Guide**, **Review**, **Ranked List** or **Comparison**.

Your own pages are marked **Owned**. Any other page has **Add as competitor**, which adds the page's domain to your tracked competitors.

## The summary cards

Five cards sum the page up:

- **Total citations**: how many times AI answers cited it in the window.
- **Answers citing**: how many answers cited it.
- **Prompts**: how many prompts those answers came from.
- **Platforms**: how many AI platforms cited it, and which.
- **Last seen**: when it was last cited, with the date it was first seen.

## Extra cards for your own pages

On a page of your own, two more cards appear:

- **Targeted in N prompts**: the prompts that list this page in their **Target URLs**, and how many times it was cited for each.
- **AI-referred visits**: visits to this page from AI platforms in the window, by platform.

## Cited in

**Cited in** lists every AI answer in the window that cites the page, newest first, 10 a page:

- **Date**: when the answer was tracked.
- **Platform**: the AI platform that gave the answer.
- **Prompt**: the prompt; click it to open the prompt's page.
- **Sentiment**: the answer's sentiment.
- **Brand mentioned**: a check when the AI answer names your brand. It says nothing about the cited page's own text.
- **Position**: where the page's first citation sits among the answer's citations: **Early**, **Mid** or **Late**, with its number, such as #2 of 7.

## Prompts breakdown

**Prompts breakdown** lists the prompts whose answers cite the page, with **Answers**, **Citations** and **Last seen**, the most citations first. Use it to see exactly which questions lead AI engines to this page.

## Use what you find

- On one of your own pages, **Cited in** and **Prompts breakdown** show the prompts AI engines cite it for, on which platforms and how often.
- A third-party page that AI engines lean on for your prompts is a place to be mentioned or cited.
- A competitor's page shows which of your prompts send AI engines to that competitor. **Add as competitor** starts tracking a site that isn't on your list yet.
