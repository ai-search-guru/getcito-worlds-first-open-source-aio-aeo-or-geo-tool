On **Opportunities**, click an opportunity's title to open it in a panel beside the list. The page's address keeps it, so a link opens it too. **Open in new tab**, in the row's **…** menu, opens it as a page of its own. The panel shows what the opportunity rests on, and every action you can take on it.

## What it rests on

**Source Data** shows the related prompt, **Est. AI Volume**, your **Current Visibility**, the **Competitor Gap**, the **Strongest Competitor**, the **Search Intent**, the keywords, the searches AI engines ran for the prompt, and the competitors cited.

An opportunity sent from an Action Center task shows that task instead: where, why, how, and when it's done.

**Results** lists the Action Center actions sent from the opportunity, with what changed once their work is complete and measured.

## For a group of prompts

An opportunity built for a group of prompts also shows:

- **Your Site**: which of your pages to work on, with their AI citations and sessions over 30 days.
- **Recommended Assets**: the main piece of work and any supporting ones. Each can go to the Action Center on its own.
- **Opportunity Basket**: its prompts, and the searches AI engines ran for them.
- **Why This Score**: demand counts 40%, the visibility gap 30%, the competitor gap 20% and intent 10%, measured over the last 30 days.

## The panel's buttons

- **Send to Actions** makes the opportunity an action in the Action Center. The button then reads **Open in Actions**.
- **Generate Brief** writes a content brief. It's for admins and managers, and once the brief is written the button reads **Brief Generated**.
- **Send to Editor** opens **New content** with the opportunity picked: a new page, or the page of yours it names to optimize. Once a draft is being written for it, the button reads **Open draft**. It's offered for owned opportunities that are still open.
- **Write this**, beside it, has the Content Agent brief and write the page in one go. See below.
- **Export** downloads the opportunity as CSV, Excel or Word.

## Write this

**Write this**, on the panel and in a row's **…** menu, takes an owned opportunity to a page to review in one go. First it says what will happen and about how much of your monthly AI usage it takes. Then:

1. The Content Agent builds a brief from the opportunity's prompts, their fan-outs and the pages AI cites for them.
2. It writes the page from the brief, by your rules and the page type's. For an opportunity on a page you have, it suggests changes to that page instead.
3. The editor opens and shows the agent at work. You review the page there, with a free estimate of its Site Audit score; nothing is published.

You can stop it from the editor. If the brief can't be built, the agent writes from what there is.

## The … menu

The panel's **…** menu holds the rest:

- **Send to Plan**, for admins and managers: the opportunity goes to the top of the Content plan. Once it's a priority there, it reads **Open in Plan**.
- **Another angle**, except on an opportunity sent from the Action Center.
- **Send to Workflow**, which reads **Send Again** once it's sent.
- **Copy All Data**.
- **Mark as reviewed**, for a new opportunity: its status becomes Reviewed.
- **Restore**, for an archived one: it comes back as New.
- **Dismiss**.

## Another angle

**Another angle**, for admins and managers, writes one more opportunity for the same need, with a different content type, format, audience or approach. You can add a direction of your own. Both opportunities stay in the list, so you keep the one you prefer.

It counts as one of the month's new opportunities.

## Dismiss an opportunity

**Dismiss**, for admins and managers, asks why:

- **The topic doesn't matter to us** keeps it from being suggested again in this form for 90 days.
- **Right topic, wrong angle** dismisses it and opens **Another angle**, so you can ask for a better take on the same need.

## Follow it to Done

Once an opportunity is in the Action Center, its status follows its action: **In Progress** once work on it starts, **Done** when it's complete. Its **Results** card shows what changed once the work has been measured.

A done opportunity comes back as **New**, marked **New signal detected**, when its score has risen since, or the visibility its work gained didn't hold.
