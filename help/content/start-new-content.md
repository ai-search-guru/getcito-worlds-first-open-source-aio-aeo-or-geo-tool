**New content** starts a page, either a new one or one you already have, written by you, the Content Agent or a teammate. It opens from the Plan and the Library, from a Plan row's button, from **Send to Editor** on an opportunity, and from **Create content** in a prompt's menu on Tracked Prompts. **Editor** in the sidebar opens it too when you have no draft to go back to, or when that draft was deleted or archived.

## Create a new page

Pick **Create from scratch** to write a new page from:

- a **Content Opportunity**;
- a **Prompt**. Prompts where AI already names or cites you in the last 30 days aren't listed, as they need no new page;
- a **Topic** that has tracked prompts.

**Send to Editor** on an opportunity opens New content with the opportunity already picked: a new page, or the page of yours it names to optimize. Earned opportunities don't go to the editor.

## Optimize a page you have

Pick **Optimize content** to work on an existing page:

- **Page URL**: a page on your brand's site. **Import** brings in its main text, headings, links and schema; menus and footers stay out.
- **Paste text** from Word, Google Docs or a web page. Headings, lists, links and tables are kept.
- **Upload file**: Word, Markdown, HTML or text, up to 10 MB.

## Pick the page type

Click **Continue**. GetCito picks a **Page type** from the title, the content, the page or the opportunity, and says which. Pick another if it's wrong; you can also change it later in the editor.

The page types on offer follow the brand's **Site type** in the brand settings: B2B SaaS, E-commerce, B2C or Mobile app. A mobile app, for example, gets **Feature page**, **Pricing** and **Help & FAQ** among its types, and no product or destination pages.

For a new page, **Overview** lists your pages AI already cites for the same prompts, with how many of those prompts each one answers, its AI citations and its Google position. Pick **Optimize … instead** on one of those pages to work on it, or **Create a new page**.

## Choose who does it

- **Me**, the default: **Open editor** opens the draft for you to write.
- **Agent**: **Start the agent**, and the Content Agent writes a new page, or suggests changes to an existing one, in the background. The editor shows it at work. You can leave: the draft waits in the Library under **In review**. It uses this month's AI usage.
- **Team**: **Send to Action Center** saves the draft in the Library and sends it to the Action Center as an action. Pick a teammate in **Assign to**, or leave it unassigned.

## If the agent can't start

If this month's AI usage is spent, there's no active subscription, or the agent is already writing another of your team's drafts, the agent doesn't start. The draft stays in the Library for you to write yourself.
