Chat keeps every chat, so you can find one again by a word in its name or in anything that was said in it. **All chats** lists them all in one place, with your archived chats.

## Search chats

Type in **Search chats** under **Chats** in the chat menu. The menu then lists the chats whose name or messages have every word you typed, newest first, in place of the days. A few letters are enough: "citat" finds "citations". Click **Clear filters**, or press Esc, to see your chats by day again. Archived chats are left out of the search.

## Search from anywhere in Chat

Press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac) anywhere in Chat to open the search box over the page; press it again to close it. It starts with **New chat** and your latest chats. Type to find chats and Chat pages such as **All chats**. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. Enter opens the first match, or starts a new chat when nothing matches. When the chat menu is hidden, the **Search** button at the top opens the same box.

## All chats

Click **All chats** at the bottom of the chat menu. **Active** lists every chat you can open: yours and the ones teammates shared with you, with the step each one is on, who started it and when it last changed. Search there looks through names and messages too. The filters show only chats that are **Running**, **Needs you** or **Done**, your own (**Mine**), or chats shared with your team (**Team**). Click a row to open its chat.

## Archive and unarchive

**Archive** in a chat's **⋯** menu takes it off the chat menu; the message that appears says where it went. To archive several at once, tick your chats in **All chats** and click **Archive selected**.

Archived chats are under **Archived** in **All chats**, or **Archived** at the bottom of the chat menu. Tick them and click **Unarchive selected** to put them back in your chat menu. You can still open an archived chat to read it.
