These options apply only when your deployment has billing enabled. Check the plans and terms shown in your workspace.

During the free trial, the AI features share a starter AI credit: Improve suggestions, the Content Editor's AI, the Action Center's task writing, and Chat where it's on. The credit is smaller than a plan's monthly AI usage, so the app shows how far along it is in words, not as a percentage.

## Where you see it

- The **AI** button at the top right of each page reads **Trial credit**, then **Almost used** from 80%, and **Used up** once it's spent. Click it to see what the credit covers and when the trial ends.
- The AI usage card in **Settings › Billing** shows the same, and so does the usage next to **Settings** in Chat's menu.
- From 80%, a note under the page header says the credit is almost used.

## Start your plan now

Once the credit is almost or all used, an admin can click **Start my plan now**: in the note under the page header, in the AI button's panel, or in the Billing card. A dialog shows the plan, what your card is charged today and that the free trial ends now. There's nothing to type: the card you added for the trial is on file.

Click **Start my plan now** in the dialog. The trial ends, the card pays the plan's first period, and the plan's full monthly AI usage starts from zero. On a monthly plan, the first-month offer applies while it's on.

## If the card is declined

Nothing changes and the trial carries on. Click **Update card** in the message to change the card, then try again.

## Members who aren't admins

Only admins can start or buy the plan. Other members see a note to ask an admin instead of the button.

## Once the credit is used up

AI features stop until the plan starts, now or when the trial ends. Chat keeps working only where it runs on your workspace's own AI key: own keys come with the Enterprise plan, and a key saved before Chat was turned on for your workspace keeps running it for 30 days. Where Chat isn't turned on yet, Agent Chat on your own AI key keeps working.
