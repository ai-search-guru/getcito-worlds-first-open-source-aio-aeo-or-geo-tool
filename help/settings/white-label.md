**White label** in Settings is your agency's kit: your logo, main color, company name and a footer line, for what you send your clients instead of GetCito's look. Each brand can also have its client's logo, for the cover of that brand's reports.

## Set up your kit

- **Logo**: click **Upload logo** and pick a PNG, SVG or JPG of up to 1 MB. It is saved as soon as it is up. **Replace** and **Remove** change it later. A wide logo on a transparent background works best.
- **Company name**: your agency's name, as your clients know it.
- **Main color**: pick it, or type its hex code, such as #1d4ed8. Leave it empty to keep GetCito's.
- **Report footer**: one line at the bottom of each page, such as your website. Leave it empty for none.

Click **Save changes**. **Preview** shows a report's first lines with your kit as you type.

## When the kit is used

The kit is used once it has a company name or a logo, on a plan with white label; the section says whether it is in use. Until then, or if the workspace's plan no longer has white label, everything keeps GetCito's look and the kit stays saved.

A light main color, such as yellow, is hard to read as text on white paper. The rules and bars keep your color, and text set in it is darkened just enough to stay readable. The section warns you when that happens.

## Where your kit appears

With the kit in use, a brand's reports carry it. The report's page opens with a cover: your logo (or your company name without one), "Prepared for" and the brand's name, and the client's logo when the brand has one.

**Download PDF** makes the PDF with the same cover, your main color on its rules and bars, your footer line at the bottom of each page, and your company name as its author. It no longer carries GetCito's footer or names GetCito as its author, and its file name starts with your company's name.

Downloads from the other pages, such as CSV files, Excel sheets and Word files, also start their names with your company's name instead of GetCito's, and a Word file names your company as its author. Emails keep GetCito's look for now.

## Who can change it

Only admins change the kit; everyone else in the workspace sees it. Under it, the section says when it was last changed and by whom.

On a plan without white label, the section is locked and names the plan that has it. Admins see **Upgrade**, which opens Billing; everyone else is asked to turn to an admin.

## Add a client logo to a brand

Open the brand's **Brand settings**. On the **General** tab, **Client logo** takes the client's logo for that brand, in the same formats as your own. It goes on the cover of the brand's reports, beside "Prepared for" and the brand's name.

Admins and managers upload, replace and remove it; everyone who can see the brand sees it. The demo brand has none.
