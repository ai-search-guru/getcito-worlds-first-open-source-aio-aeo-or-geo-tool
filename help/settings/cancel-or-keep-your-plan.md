These options apply only when your deployment has billing enabled. Check the plans and terms shown in your workspace.

Canceling doesn't end your plan at once: it runs to the end of the period you've paid for, and every page says when it ends. Until then you can keep it with one click.

## Cancel

In **Settings › Billing**, an admin clicks **Cancel Subscription**. A short form asks what went wrong; both the reasons and the message are optional. You can also choose to give the team a few days to put it right, and your plan stays as it is meanwhile. **Send and cancel** cancels the plan at the end of the current period.

## While the plan runs out

A thin band at the top of every page reads **Your plan ends on** and the date. For a canceled free trial, it says the trial ends on that date and the plan won't start.

An admin can click **Keep my plan** in the band: the plan renews as before, and the band goes. **Reactivate Subscription** in **Settings › Billing** does the same. Other members see a note to ask an admin.

The band's close button hides it in that browser tab. It comes back in a new tab, after the browser is closed, and when the plan is canceled to another date.

## When the plan has ended

Your workspace becomes read-only: you can look around, but adding, running and AI need a plan. Choose one from **See plans**.
