Everyone in your workspace has one of four roles: **Admin**, **Manager**, **Analyst** or **Agency partner**. Admins set roles in **Settings › Team**. Where Chat is on, **Roles & access** in Settings lists each person's role, with a table of what each role can do in the app and in Chat. A button your role can't use isn't shown.

## What everyone can do

Every role reads the dashboards, prompts, citations, mentions and reports, and asks Chat. The daily work is everyone's too:

- the **Action Center**: sending findings to it, and working on actions and tasks;
- writing in the **Content Editor**;
- running a **Site Audit**.

Agency partners can do what Analysts can.

## What admins and managers do

Admins and Managers also:

- add and change prompts, topics and competitors, and the prompts' locations;
- run tracking now, and analyze search volumes;
- generate suggestions, opportunities, briefs, Improve and tasks, which use the plan's AI allowance;
- send to your webhook and your CMS, and archive or delete;
- approve brand facts, and change a brand's settings and connections.

Through Chat, they make changes to the workspace, each after a card they confirm.

## What only admins do

Only Admins change billing and the plan, invite people, change roles and remove people, and set Chat's rules and the AI provider keys. They can let Managers change menus in **Menu access**.
