Each person can say what they work on or lead. Their left menu then keeps only those areas' sections. Pick one or more; picking none keeps every section. **Settings** is always on the menu, with your API keys and MCP.

## The work areas

- **Founders & leaders**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Mentions, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.
- **AEO/GEO & Audit**: Action Center, Agent Center, Content, Audit, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports.
- **Content**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Content, Prompts, Citations, Reports.
- **Brand & PR**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Mentions, Citations, Reports, Competitors.
- **Growth**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Prompts, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.
- **Product Development**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Product Visibility, Reports.
- **Business Development**: Action Center, Chat, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Citations, Reports, Competitors.
- **Developers**: Action Center, Agent Center, Audit, Prompts, Citations, AI Referral Traffic, Reports.
- **Analytics**: Action Center, Agent Center, Brand Visibility, Prompts, Mentions, Citations, Product Visibility, AI Referral Traffic, Reports, Competitors.

## When you're asked

**What do you work on?** is asked once:

- when you sign up, after your competitors and before you choose a plan;
- when you join from an invitation, on your first sign-in;
- on a self-hosted install, the first time the admin opens the dashboard.

**Skip: show me everything** keeps every section.

## Change your work areas

Change your answer any time in **Settings › Account › Your work areas**. It also shows your menu, and why a section is off it: **Hidden by your admin** or **Not in your work areas**.

When something is off your menu, the bottom of the sidebar says how much, such as **3 sections off your menu**, with **Change** leading to your work areas.
