The **Perception** tab of the Mentions & Sentiment page shows the words AI uses next to your brand, and how they compare with the words it uses for your competitors. The terms are taken from the sentences that name each brand by fixed rules, not by an AI model. A sentence that names two brands lends its words to both.

## What AI associates with your brand

The first card lists the words and phrases that appear next to your brand, ranked by the number of responses they appear in, each with its change from the previous period. Hover over a count to see its share of the responses naming you.

## Brand shape and Brand × attribute

**Brand shape: you vs top competitors** is a radar chart of your brand and your three most-mentioned competitors, on the attributes that tell them apart. It needs at least three attributes and at least one competitor named in the period. **Brand × attribute** shows the same attributes as a heat map: each attribute's share of the responses naming each brand, for you and up to four competitors.

## Biggest gap

Under the brand shape, **Biggest gap** names the attribute where one of the competitors on the brand shape leads you by the widest margin, when that margin is at least 20 points. For example, AI may use *affordable* in 45% of the responses naming a competitor but in only 10% of the responses naming you. A competitor's figures marked **Low sample** are left out, and the note doesn't show while yours are.

## Frequently mentioned with

This card lists the competitors named in the same responses as you, with the change from the previous period. It shows whether the set of brands AI considers alongside you is changing.
